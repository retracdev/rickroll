# Retrac's Rickroll

**An open-source Rickroll that's never gonna give you up.**

Ever wanted to Rickroll your friends in a special way? Well, now's your chance!

**Retrac's Rickroll** takes the classic internet prank to another level with creative, unexpected, and entertaining ways to Rickroll just about anyone.

Whether you're looking for a traditional Rickroll or something a little more elaborate, we've got you covered.

## Features

- **Multiple Rickroll variations** — Why settle for one way to Rickroll someone?
- **Creative disguises** — Surprise your friends with websites that aren't quite what they seem.
- **Open source** — Explore the code, contribute your own ideas, or build something new.
- **Free to use** — No subscriptions, no paywalls. Just Rickrolls.

## Contributing

Got an idea for a new Rickroll? We'd love to see it!

Retrac's Rickroll is open-source. No matter if you are fixing a bug, creating a new variation, or adding another feature, just create a PR and I'll check it out!

Or, you can [fork this repository](https://github.com/retracdev/rickroll/fork) and edit it yourself!
